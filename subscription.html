FAQ

Common questions.

Will my athlete get a scholarship?

No platform can promise that. ScholarPath is built around the five things that put a scholarship within reach: preparation, exposure, eligibility, character, and college fit.

What if we can't afford the subscription?

Spark is free forever, and Equity Access Codes from sponsors and nonprofits unlock paid features for athletes who qualify.

Is my child's data safe?

Parental consent is required for under-18 athletes. We're SOC 2-aligned, FERPA-aligned, COPPA-compliant, and recruiters are identity-verified before access.

How does Student Loader work?

Friends or siblings can build a profile for an athlete only after verified consent from the athlete and a parent. Loaders can never message college coaches — that stays athlete-only.
