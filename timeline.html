Class of 2027 · Roadmap

Your recruiting timeline.

A grade-by-grade plan from freshman year through signing day. Tasks marked ● are due now; ✓ are done; ○ are coming up.

9

Freshman: Foundation

Set the academic and athletic foundation. Recruiting starts with eligibility, not exposure.

Fall

  • Verify NCAA core-course list with counselor
  • Set GPA target above division minimums
  • Build ScholarPath profile (basic)

Winter

  • Capture first highlight clips
  • Establish study habits and time blocking

Spring + Summer

  • Attend a regional skills clinic
  • Lift program with school or club trainer
  • Read 1 book on college recruiting (parent + athlete)
10

Sophomore: Development

Build the athletic foundation, leadership, and exposure baseline. Many programs begin watching this year.

Fall

  • Capture full-game film
  • Take the leadership module on ScholarPath
  • Identify 25 target schools across all divisions

Winter

  • Combine baseline (40, vert, agility)
  • First highlight reel with AI auto-tagging
  • Update ScholarPath profile to 80%+ complete

Summer

  • First ID camp at a target school
  • Open NCAA Eligibility Center account
11

Junior: Exposure

You are here. The most active recruiting year. Compete, communicate, and convert interest into evaluations.

Fall

  • Strong junior season starts (game-day routines)
  • Send introductory emails to 25 target schools
  • Attend 1 verified showcase

Winter

  • Mid-year transcript to NCAA Eligibility Center
  • Begin unofficial campus visits
  • Refresh AI-edited highlight reel with playoff footage

Spring

  • Take SAT/ACT (if test-required programs are on your list)
  • Confirm summer camp schedule (3–4 verified events)
  • Voice interview practice — 1 session per week

Summer

  • Attend ID camps at top 5 fits
  • Verbal interest from coaches → unofficial visits
  • Update profile with verified camp results
12

Senior: Decision & Transition

Compare offers honestly. Sign confidently. Prepare for college life.

Fall

  • Final transcript prep with counselor
  • 5 official visits (D-I window opens Aug 1)
  • Submit FAFSA October 1
  • Apply to all schools on your list

Winter

  • Compare offers — full vs partial, athletic + merit + need
  • Use ScholarPath offer comparison tool
  • Discuss with parents, counselor, and high school coach

Spring

  • Sign National Letter of Intent
  • Signing day recognition at school
  • Final transcript to NCAA Eligibility Center
  • Connect with future teammate (alumni mentor on ScholarPath)

This roadmap calibrates to your sport, division target, and graduation year. Update your profile to refresh.
