Required for athletes under 18

Parent / Guardian Consent

A parent or legal guardian must review and sign before an under-18 athlete profile is published or visible to recruiters.

What you're consenting to

  • → Your athlete may publish a profile visible to identity-verified college recruiters.
  • → Athletic film, stats, transcripts, and eligibility status may be displayed when authorized.
  • → Communications between recruiters and your athlete are audit-logged. You will receive a copy of every recruiter message.
  • → You can revoke consent at any time, removing your athlete's profile from recruiter visibility within 24 hours.
  • → ScholarPath does not sell student data. See Privacy Policy.
