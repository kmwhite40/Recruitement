Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Plain-language summary first, full details below.

In short

  • → We collect only what's needed to help athletes get recruited and stay eligible.
  • → Parental consent is required for any user under 18.
  • → We never sell student data.
  • → Recruiters only see what athletes (and their schools) authorize.
  • → All recruiter accounts are identity-verified.
  • → You can export, modify, or delete your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

Profile data (name, school, sport, position, height, weight), academic data (transcripts, GPA, NCAA core-course pace), film and stats, communications, device and usage data, and payment information when you subscribe.

2. How We Use It

To build athlete profiles, calculate eligibility and readiness, surface verified athletes to identity-verified recruiters, deliver outreach with athlete consent, and provide academic and financial-aid education.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Users under 13 are not permitted without verified parental consent. For users 13–17, parental consent is required before profile publishing or recruiter visibility.

4. Education Records (FERPA)

When a school chooses to share transcript data through ScholarPath, the school remains the controller of those records and the athlete (or parent) controls visibility. We act as a school official under FERPA where applicable.

5. Data Sharing

We share data with: (a) verified college recruiters at your direction; (b) your school, club, parents, and counselors with your consent; (c) processors that help us run the service (storage, payments, video infrastructure) under strict contracts. We do not sell personal information.

6. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging on every mutating action, identity verification for adults contacting minors, granular privacy controls, and a published subprocessor list. We target SOC 2 Type II.

7. Your Rights

Access, correct, export, and delete your data at any time. Revoke loader and recruiter access in one tap. Submit complaints through your account or to privacy@scholarpath.example.

8. Contact

privacy@scholarpath.example · ScholarPath Athletics, Trust & Safety Team.
